Behind the counter

Every café has someone who knows the recipes by heart. Here's mine — the short version.

How this started

I'm a Computer Science undergraduate at PUCIT, currently in my fourth semester. Competitive programming is the part of CS I keep coming back to — I like it for the same reason I like a well-plotted novel: there's always a structure hiding underneath, and finding it is the whole point.

Where this is headed

I'm training toward ICPC regionals, which means building up both breadth (covering the standard technique list properly) and depth (actually being fast and correct under contest pressure). This site exists because I needed somewhere to keep that process honest — a running record of what I've studied and what I've actually tried, instead of just planning to get to it eventually.

How I practice

Mostly Codeforces for contest-format practice, and LeetCode for topic-focused grinding between rounds. The Topics and Problem Log pages on this site are where the two connect — every problem I log gets tagged back to the topic it's testing, so I can see patterns in what's actually giving me trouble.

Why "Anteiku Algorithms"

Anteiku is the café that hides something sharper beneath a calm, everyday surface. That felt like the right metaphor for a training log: a quiet, unglamorous place where the real work — the stuff that doesn't show up in a highlight reel — actually gets tracked.
